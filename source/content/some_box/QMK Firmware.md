This is a keyboard firmware based on the [tmk_keyboard firmware](http://github.com/tmk/tmk_keyboard) with some useful features for Atmel AVR and ARM controllers, and more specifically, the [OLKB product line](http://olkb.com), the [ErgoDox EZ](http://www.ergodox-ez.com) keyboard, the [Moonlander](https://www.zsa.io/moonlander/) keyboard, and the [Clueboard product line](http://clueboard.co/).

I used it in my [Redox keyboard](https://github.com/mattdibi/redox-keyboard) the one I called [[Monochromatic MKI]] because it's white with black keycaps :3
