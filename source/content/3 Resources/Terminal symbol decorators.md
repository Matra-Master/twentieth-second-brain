Talking with chat I found out that kitty may modify some glyphs and make them look the same as others.
My tmux config had these "◤◥" but they looked more stretched and that made my tmux status bar look nice on kitty but horrible on any other terminal.
I also have a list from chat

| Symbol | Unicode | Typical Use          | Description                      |
| ------ | ------- | -------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| ``    | U+E0B0  | Right separator      | Triangle pointing right          |
| ``    | U+E0B1  | Right thin separator | Thin right angle separator       |
| ``    | U+E0B2  | Left separator       | Triangle pointing left           |
| ``    | U+E0B3  | Left thin separator  | Thin left angle separator        |
| ``    | U+E0B4  | Right rounded        | Curved right separator           |
| ``    | U+E0B5  | Right thin rounded   | Curved thin right separator      |
| ``    | U+E0B6  | Left rounded         | Curved left separator            |
| ``    | U+E0B7  | Left thin rounded    | Curved thin left separator       |
| ``    | U+E0B8  | Right bold triangle  | Wide triangle                    |
| ``    | U+E0BA  | Left bold triangle   | Inverse of ``, points left      |
| ``    | U+E0BB  | Left thin triangle   | Narrow version of ``            |
| ``    | U+E0B9  | Right thin triangle  | Narrow version of ``            |
| ``    | U+E0BE  |                      | Inverse of ``, points down      |
| ``    | U+E0BC  |                      | Inverse of ``, points down      |
